Crazy Circus Maze 3D: Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Crazy Circus Maze 3D (the "Game") handles and why. The Game is published on Google Play under the developer account livingexcellencegames by 33EXE STUDIOS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us").

In short: the Game has no ads. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and we never sell or share your information for advertising.

1. Information kept on your device

Your progress (the levels you've finished, your stars and your best times) and your settings are saved on your phone. They stay there and are removed when you uninstall the Game.

2. Your phone's motion sensor

You steer the ball by tilting your phone, so the Game reads your phone's motion sensor while you play. This is used on your phone only, to move the ball; it is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

3. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the Game with your Play Games player ID and display name so that features such as achievements and cloud saves work. Your progress can be stored in your Google account's saved games so it follows you to a new phone. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

4. Crash reports

To find and fix bugs, the Game uses Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service: if the Game crashes, a crash report with the technical state of the device and the Game at the time (for example, device model, Android version and app version) is sent, linked to a random installation ID, not your name or Google account. It is used only to fix problems, processed by Google on our behalf (see Firebase privacy information), and deleted automatically after 90 days.

5. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks and no advertising ID use.
  • No gameplay analytics or tracking of how you play (crash reports, above, are only used to fix crashes).
  • No selling, renting or trading of any information.
  • No collection of your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • No in-app purchases at present. If optional purchases are added later, payments are handled entirely by Google Play, we never see your payment details, and this policy will be updated first.

6. Children

The Game's content is family-friendly, but the Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security and where data is stored

Information handled by Google Play Games and Firebase is stored on Google's servers, which may be outside your country, and is protected by Google's security measures. Data sent by the Game is encrypted in transit.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the Game removes everything stored on your phone.
  • You can remove the Game's Play Games data from your Google account at any time (Play Games app or myaccount.google.com).
  • You can ask us to access or delete crash data linked to your installation by emailing us; we will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU GDPR), you may have further rights, and we will honour them.

9. How to delete your data

To delete your data from Crazy Circus Maze 3D (developer: 33EXE STUDIOS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED, Google Play account livingexcellencegames):

  1. Progress and settings on your phone: uninstall the Game, or clear its storage in your phone's settings (Settings > Apps > Crazy Circus Maze 3D > Storage > Clear storage). They are deleted at once.
  2. Google Play Games data (your achievements and cloud save for this Game): open your Play Games profile (or the Play Games app), find Crazy Circus Maze 3D and choose to delete its data. Google deletes it.
  3. Crash reports: email livingexcellencegames@gmail.com with the subject "Crazy Circus Maze 3D data deletion". We delete the crash reports we can link to your installation and reply within 30 days. Crash reports are also deleted automatically after 90 days.

Nothing is kept after these steps: the Game has no accounts of its own and keeps no other data about you.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's update notes.
